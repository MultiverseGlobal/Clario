# Pseudonyms

> *Bounded cognitive intelligences. Not replacements. Extensions.*

---

## What This Is

Pseudonyms are personal cognitive prosthetics built for one person: **Benjamin**.

They are not products. They are not assistants. They are not general-purpose AI wrappers.

Each Pseudonym embodies one dominant cognitive faculty and refuses to become anything else. Their power comes entirely from that refusal.

Just as glasses extend vision without replacing eyes — Pseudonyms extend cognition without replacing the person.

---

## The Grand Principle

Every Pseudonym answers exactly one class of questions.

| Pseudonym | Question | Faculty |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Metaphor** | *Why? Who? What does this mean?* | Identity · Memory · Context |
| **Atlas** | *How? What strategy gives the best outcome?* | Planning · Systems · Strategy |
| **William** | *What should happen now?* | Focus · Execution · Action |

Together they form one cognitive pipeline:

```
Identity
    ↓
Memory
    ↓
Context
    ↓
Reason
    ↓
Strategy
    ↓
Execution
    ↓
Action
```

The human remains sovereign at every step.

## The Three Pseudonyms

### Metaphor — The Context Keeper
The persistent layer. Carries identity, goals, history, preferences, relationships, and knowledge across every application and every AI session. Without Metaphor, every interaction starts from zero. With Metaphor, every interaction begins with continuity.

**Folder:** [`./Metaphor/`](./Metaphor/)

---

### Atlas — The Strategist
Transforms objectives into executable strategies. Identifies the dominant constraint, proposes plans, evaluates trade-offs, and adapts continuously as reality changes. Atlas is not Benjamin's memory — it is his strategist.

**Location:** `c:\Users\SUDO\Documents\Atlas io\` *(Benjamin's first public company)*

---

### William — The Commander
Closes the gap between intention and action. Continuously determines the highest-leverage next action, protects focus, manages recovery, and ensures execution. William does not decide purpose. It executes strategy.

**Folder:** [`./William/`](./William/)

## Strategy

**Build depth before breadth.**
Metaphor and William must be real before any new Pseudonym is named.

**Atlas is the proof of concept.**
The Pseudonym system exists to grow Atlas. Every tool here serves that mission.

**Private by design.**
Pseudonyms are not built for users. They are built for Benjamin. They scale inward before they scale outward.

**Discipline over features.**
A Pseudonym that does everything is not a Pseudonym. The constraint is the design.
