# Metaphor Complete Route Map

## 1. Public & Onboarding Routes

- **`/`**: Public Landing Page ("Make your AI tools work as one").
- **`/onboard`**: Redirects automatically to `/onboard/step-1`.
- **`/onboard/step-1`**: Step 1 — "What are you working on?" (project name scope).
- **`/onboard/step-2`**: Step 2 — Participant selection with authentic vector brand marks (ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub, Notion, Antigravity, Cursor).
- **`/signup`**: Step 3 — Workspace summary, connected tools list, credentials form, and immediate launch into `/world`.
- **`/login`**: Sign-in page with floating glass capsule and bare underline credentials.

## 2. Authenticated Core Routes (`(app)` group)

All authenticated pages share the glass navigation capsule (`FloatingNav`):

- **`/world` (Main Home)**: **Your Connected World**.
  - Active coordination mesh.
  - Participants health matrix (latencies, roles, status).
  - "While you were away" activity digest.
  - Pending handoff approval action.
  - Live movement stream.
- **`/tools`**: **Tools & Capabilities**.
  - Minimal registry of all connected models and tools.
  - Permissions model and inspection drawers.
- **`/handoffs`**: **Handoffs Ledger**.
  - Editorial timeline of context transfers, PR handoffs, and payload inspections.
- **`/context`**: **Context Query Engine**.
  - Bare underline query bar ("Explore your context.").
  - Synthesized response in Cormorant Garamond display font.
  - Grounding provenance facts and decision records.
- **`/connections`**: **Connections & Infrastructure**.
  - MCP (Model Context Protocol) server registry.
  - OAuth credentials and API key management.
- **`/settings`**: Workspace preferences, trust model, and account management.
